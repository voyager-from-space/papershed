papershed Source

Your LaTeX source says more than your paper does.

Comments, old drafts, a photo's GPS position, a password you typed into a % line. Drop the project zip you are about to post, for example to arXiv. papershed strips comments, drops files the paper never uses, removes image metadata and flags what looks like a leak. You get a clean zip back. It all runs in this tab; nothing is uploaded.
